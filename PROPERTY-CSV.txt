PROPERTY CSV — EDITING GUIDE

IN THE APP
Banker: House rules > Customize properties > Download property CSV.
Open it in Excel, LibreOffice, Numbers or a plain-text editor.
Save as comma-separated CSV UTF-8, keeping the column headers.
Choose the edited file, click Validate & preview, then Apply property sheet.
Imports are all-or-nothing. Invalid rows display a row number and explanation.
No client installation is required; the banker imports through the browser.

WHEN TO IMPORT
Import before purchasing any properties, or reset the game first.
Owned properties, auctions, and pending trades block imports.
Reset is never performed automatically. Import preserves player balances,
player identities, and house rules. It replaces the whole property catalog.
A private game backup is created before a successful import.

WHERE DATA LIVES
Variants/<variant name>/properties.csv is the editable starter catalog.
Normal is used for new saves; the banker can choose another variant during
initial setup. There are no hardcoded property names, prices or rent schedules
in server.py anymore. Existing saves preserve a snapshot of their edition,
including imports, across restarts and resets. Editing properties.csv does
not silently change a saved game. Use the in-app import to update that game.
Download property CSV exports the CURRENT edition, including name edits.
Normal contains standard property names. Hello Kitty and Friends contains
your supplied themed names, with Electric and Water retained. Both preserve
the existing purchase prices, rent schedules, and building costs. Each
variant has its own CSV, so editions can use different values.

COLUMNS
id: unique stable identifier; letters, digits, hyphens, underscores only.
name: property name, 1–60 characters. Commas and quotes are supported through
normal CSV quoting. Spreadsheet formulas and control characters are rejected.
kind: street, railroad, or utility.
group: brown, light_blue, pink, orange, red, yellow, green, dark_blue for
streets; railroad for railroads; utility for utilities.
purchase_price: price paid to the bank.
mortgage_value: cash received when mortgaging; cannot exceed purchase_price.
house_cost: price for EACH of houses 1–4 (street only).
hotel_cost: ADDITIONAL cash for upgrading four houses to a hotel (street only).
set_multiplier: base-rent multiplier when owner holds the complete group
and the property has no buildings (normally 2; street only).
rent_base: street rent without a complete group or buildings.
rent_1_house, rent_2_houses, rent_3_houses, rent_4_houses, rent_hotel:
TOTAL rent at each building level, not amounts to add to base rent.
railroad_rent_1 through railroad_rent_4: rent for that deed when the owner
holds 1, 2, 3 or 4 railroads respectively.
utility_multiplier_1 and utility_multiplier_2: multiply dice total by these
values when the owner holds 1 or 2 utilities respectively.

All numeric values must be nonnegative whole numbers, maximum 1000000.
Do not include dollar signs, thousands separators or spreadsheet formulas.
Leave unused numeric columns blank: railroad columns for streets, etc.
Street house/hotel rent columns must be filled even if their value is zero.
Up to 128 deeds, at most 4 railroads and 2 utilities. Names and IDs must be
unique. Street groups use the classic eight colors; custom group names are
not supported. Group membership is defined by the group column.

CALCULATIONS
House sales refund half house_cost (rounded down). Downgrading a hotel to
four houses refunds half hotel_cost. A hotel's net-worth building value is
4 * house_cost + hotel_cost. Mortgage release interest stays in House rules
and is applied to mortgage_value. Supply limits remain 32 houses/12 hotels.
Game actions always calculate amounts on the server using the current sheet.
