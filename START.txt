THE BANK — X-opoly

RUN
Requires Python 3.10 or newer. No downloads or Python packages are required.
Open a terminal in this folder and run:
  python3 server.py
On Windows:
  py server.py
Or use start.sh (Linux/macOS) or start.bat (Windows).

Open the Host / banker URL printed in the terminal and enter your player name.
Give other players only the Players on your Wi-Fi URL. They open it in any
modern browser on the same local network. Keep this computer awake and the
server running. If needed, allow inbound TCP port 8000 through your firewall.
The app is for a trusted local network; it is not an internet-hosted service.
Use the same address and browser to retain your player identity.

PLAY
The host is also a player and can make bank payments and edit house rules.
Set starting cash before others join. Changes affect new players only; use
bank payments to adjust players already seated.

Banking: send money, pay fixed or percentage tax, collect the
optional Free Parking pot. Only the banker can pay GO salaries, including
landing-on-GO bonuses, to a selected player. Board movement, dice, card instructions, and turn
order stay at your physical board. Players trigger transactions manually;
balances and ownership update automatically for everyone.

Market (banker only): select an unowned deed and the player who landed there,
then sells it to them at face value or opens an auction for all players.
Players cannot buy unowned deeds without the banker.
Properties: shows only your deeds; mortgage/unmortgage, buy or sell houses
and hotels. Mortgaged deeds appear below regular deeds.
Rent: other players' deeds grouped by owner. Cards show the current rent
due (not purchase price), including buildings and ownership bonuses.
Utilities show the dice multiplier; enter the dice total to preview payment.
The payment button displays the exact amount and recipient. A changed
rent or owner is checked again by the server before money is transferred.
House rules > Customize properties (banker only): select any of the 28
properties and save its new name, regardless of who owns it.
A color group must have no buildings to trade or mortgage any of its deeds.
The app enforces 32 houses and 12 hotels and optionally even building.

Auctions: only the banker can start an auction, including for owned deeds. Opening bid is $10. Tap Bid $10 or add $1/$5/$10/$25/$100.
The clock starts immediately and resets on every accepted bid. Set Auction
time (seconds) in House rules: 1–300 seconds, default 5. Only the banker
can change it. New auctions use the saved duration; running auctions keep
their original duration for the opening countdown and every bid.
The current highest bidder is locked out until another player bids. This
also applies to the banker. Blocked bids do not restart the clock or flash.
An auction with no bids expires without a sale. Winning bids reserve cash.
The server settles the winner automatically, even when browsers close.
Mortgaged deeds must be released before auctioning. Sellers cannot bid.

Trades: offer any number of properties and money in both directions.
The recipient accepts or rejects; the sender can withdraw. Offers do not
reserve assets. Acceptance rechecks cash, ownership, mortgage/building state,
and auction locks. Failed trades move nothing. Mortgages remain attached;
this version waives immediate mortgage-transfer interest. Release interest
still applies when unmortgaging. Trades use gross cash amounts: both parties
must be able to pay their offered amounts before credits are applied.

House rules: starting cash, GO salary, double landing-on-GO salary,
Free Parking tax pot, negative balances, even building, mortgage interest,
percentage tax rate, cash/net-worth tax base, and rounding up/down.
Net worth uses purchase value for unmortgaged properties, mortgage value
for mortgaged properties, and purchase cost for buildings.

SAVING AND NEW GAMES
Every successful action automatically saves to game.json. Restarting the
server resumes it, including player sessions and any expired auction.
Keep game.json private: it includes player sessions and the host key.
To back up, stop the server with Ctrl+C and copy game.json.
To start a separate new game without overwriting this one:
  python3 server.py --data another-game.json
Use --port 8080 if port 8000 is already in use.
To restore banker access in another browser, open the complete Host / banker
URL printed in the server terminal. If already joined, that player gains
banker controls; otherwise enter your existing name to resume that player.
You can also click Banker access and paste the banker link or key. On the
join screen, choose your existing player. Money and deeds are preserved.
Keep the banker link private; the player invite link cannot grant access.
For ordinary players, if browser storage is cleared, there is no self-service identity recovery;
the host can reconcile balances/properties using payments and trades with
a replacement player. Keep the original browser session where possible.

PLAYER PIECES
Choose a piece when joining; click your name/icon in the header to edit your name or change your piece.
Each piece is exclusive to one player. Choices cover current and classic
sets: top hat, race car, battleship, Scottie dog, cat, rubber duck, penguin,
T-Rex, thimble, boot, iron, wheelbarrow, horse & rider, money bag, cannon.
Icons are bundled for offline use; attribution is in ICON-CREDITS.txt.

TABLE MANAGEMENT
House rules > Manage table offers banker-only Kick player and Reset game.
Kick revokes the player session, returns deeds to the bank without buildings
or mortgages, removes their trades, and cancels involved auctions. Kicked
players can rejoin; this is not a permanent ban. Bankers cannot be kicked.
Reset requires typing RESET. Other players are disconnected; the initiating
banker stays with starting cash. Rules and custom names are preserved;
ownership, buildings, mortgages, trades, auctions, pot and ledger are reset.
Both actions save private recovery copies under backups/ beside game.json.
To recover, stop the server and copy the desired backup over game.json.
Do not share backups: they contain player sessions.

PROPERTY EDITIONS
Starter definitions live in Variants/<variant name>/properties.csv. See PROPERTY-CSV.txt
for columns and import/export instructions. Banker controls are under
House rules > Customize properties > Property spreadsheet. Imported sheets
are saved with the game and survive resets.

QR INVITATIONS
Click the Invite your players address to display a QR code. It contains
only the local join URL, never the banker key. Players scan it on the same
Wi-Fi. QR generation is local; no external QR service is contacted.

TAX SPACES
Banking has fixed Income Tax ($200) and Luxury Tax ($100) buttons. The
Hello Kitty and Friends variant calls the latter Renovation. The server enforces those amounts regardless of percentage
tax settings. The optional Free Parking rule also collects these payments.

SHUTDOWN
Run ./shutdown.sh (Linux/macOS), shutdown.bat (Windows), or:
  python3 shutdown.py
This saves and stops only the X-opoly server, not the computer. Run it
on the banker computer from this app's folder. No admin/sudo required.
For a custom server, use the same --port and --data values:
  python3 shutdown.py --port 8080 --data another-game.json
The shutdown script requires the updated server.py. If an older server
is already running, stop it with Ctrl+C once and start the updated version.
Future stops can use the shutdown script. Existing game data is preserved.
Restart with start.sh/start.bat to resume. Auction deadlines are preserved;
an expired auction settles when the game resumes.

BID FLASHES
Accepted bids flash the auction panel orange for the bidder and white for
all other players. Live server events share a timestamp to synchronize
connected browsers; network latency or background tabs can delay display.
Reduced-motion preferences use a colored outline instead of a full-panel
flash. Rejected bids do not flash. No client installation is needed.

BANKRUPTCY
Banking > Declare bankruptcy requires typing BANKRUPT and confirming.
Only a player can declare their own bankruptcy. Their cash becomes $0; all
their properties return to the bank with mortgages and buildings cleared.
Pending trades and auctions involving them are cancelled. They remain
visible as Bankrupt and can watch but cannot participate until a new game.
A bankrupt banker retains table management and banking for other players.
A private recovery copy is saved under backups/ before bankruptcy.

END OF GAME AND MOBILE PLAY
After a bankruptcy leaves one active player (with at least two players at
the table), everyone sees a winner screen. Gameplay stops; the banker can
use Start a new game to reset with confirmation. Results survive restarts.
Phones use bottom navigation, larger tap targets, horizontally scrolling
player balances, and two-column property cards. Mortgaged properties are
grouped below regular properties.
Banking > Pay $50 · Get out of jail pays your own fee to the bank and records
it in the ledger. It does not contribute to Free Parking.
Tap your name/icon in the header to rename yourself. Names must be unique
and 1–32 characters. Renaming preserves your money, properties, and login.

VARIANTS AND INITIAL SETUP
New saves use Normal: standard property names and classic player pieces.
Open the Host / banker link and join. Go to House rules > Customize properties > Game variant. Choose a folder
name and click Apply variant. This works after other players join, until
gameplay starts. Then tap your player icon to choose
a piece. All players receive the selected variant; classic pieces remain
available with every variant.

Add editions under Variants/<edition name>/. Each immediate subfolder is
automatically listed by name. It must contain properties.csv with the same
headers as the Normal template. Optional PNGs go directly in that folder;
the filename without .png becomes the piece label. Use valid PNGs under
5 MB each. Reference files in deeper subfolders are not player icons.
No manifest or source-code changes are required. Refresh the browser after
adding a folder or PNG. Invalid variants show an explanation and cannot be
selected. Folder names and filenames are case-sensitive on Linux.

Included: Normal, Classic U.S. Edition, and Hello Kitty and Friends
(themed property names only). No character artwork is included.
Optional local PNG icons are supported and appear before classic pieces.
Variant selection copies the CSV into the save. Editing a folder does not
silently replace a running game's values. Reset to return to setup; the
previous variant remains selected until the banker applies another one.

UPGRADING
The app folder is now x-opoly. Stop the old server, extract the new folder,
and copy your existing game.json (and optionally backups/) into it before
starting. Keep using the same browser and URL to retain your login. Existing
property names, balances, mortgages, buildings, and sessions are preserved.
Older themed saves retain their theme; brand-new games default to Normal.
Older CSV headers are accepted; new exports use set_multiplier.
